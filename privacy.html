TARIK · DATA NOTICE

Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

1. Owner and scope

Tarık is provided by Ömer Faruk Köken. Contact tarikkayet@gmail.com for privacy questions and data requests. This document describes product data flows; it is not a completed Turkish KVKK compliance assessment or a legal certification.

Quran reading, bookmarks, lessons and the library do not require an account. This version is free, with no ads, tracking SDKs, paid features, subscriptions or real-money in-app purchases.

2. Local learning data

Lesson progress, review plans, goals, streaks, earned rewards, preferences, Quran reading position, bookmarks, favourites and library reading records are stored on the device. They are not automatically uploaded. Other people sharing the device may see the local profile.

Learning and reading activity can reveal sensitive information about religious interests and habits. If you export a file backup, you choose its destination and recipients. Operating-system device backups are separate from Tarık’s optional cloud progress backup.

3. Accounts and Google / Apple sign-in

If you sign in, Supabase Auth processes your email, account identifier and session information. Google sign-in requests only openid email profile. Basic profile details, including a name or profile image if provided, may be passed to the account service. Your Google password is not given to Tarık. When enabled, Apple sign-in requests name and email and can use Apple’s private email relay; your Apple password is not given to Tarık either. Apple sign-in still requires pre-release configuration and validation and is not enabled in every current build.

The session is retained on the device. Authentication providers may process connection information such as your IP address. Signing in does not enable progress backup automatically.

4. Social features and optional cloud backup

Account and social features are limited in this version to users who declare they are at least 18. This is a declaration, not identity verification or verified parental consent. Supabase stores the nickname, age-band declaration, community-rules choice, friendships, blocks, reports, league records and shared goals. Relevant friends and league members can see the nickname and relevant scores, not the email address.

If a league or shared goal is active, a lesson identifier and start/completion times may be sent to the server. A shared-goal friend can see study-day counts, not your audio or recognised text. Library reading records are not part of the cloud progress backup.

Only after separate consent and choosing “Back up this device” in Profile → Progress backup are lesson history, review plans and Quran reading position uploaded. One current backup is kept per account; there is no automatic device sync. Audio, measurement records and location are excluded. The cloud backup can be deleted separately.

5. Microphone and listening to your own recitation

The microphone is requested when you start a voice activity. On supported devices recognition uses the on-device route; there is no fallback to cloud recognition if that route is unavailable. Initial language-asset setup may require connectivity. Offline recognition out of the box on every device is not guaranteed. Raw audio is not uploaded to Tarık, Supabase or the progress backup; live recognition does not persist raw audio.

The separate optional “Listen to myself” tool holds a temporary recording only in device memory, without writing it to disk or a server. Leaving the screen or backgrounding the app releases the microphone and recording. It is not scored. Word-text matching is not proof of correct tajweed, articulation, vowels or pronunciation.

6. Optional local family-test measurements

After technical tools are separately unlocked, collection must be explicitly enabled. It is off by default. Recognised text, target verse identifiers, provider information, result counts, latency and reported device information stay on the device. Reading scenario, reader age group, pace, environment and airplane mode are user declarations, not app observations. No raw audio is stored.

The limit is 20 records / 128 KB; when full, new records are not added. JSONL export requires an explicit action. Measurements are excluded from Supabase, account exports and file/cloud progress backups. Turning off collection or hiding technical tools does not erase records. Delete them in the measurement screen or through Settings → Delete learning data on this device.

7. Prayer times, location and Qibla

Prayer times are calculated on the device from the selected city/district centre or saved approximate coordinates obtained with permission. No prayer-time request is sent to AlAdhan or another calendar API; coordinates are not sent externally for this purpose. Calculations use AlAdhan method 13 parameters and are not an official Diyanet timetable. The initial prayer-location coverage is Türkiye, using the Istanbul time zone.

Manual city selection needs no location permission. Choosing “Use my location” saves an approximate place locally; Prayer times → Forget saved location removes it. The separately started Qibla compass processes live position and heading locally without recording or uploading them. It stops when leaving the screen or backgrounding the app. There is no continuous background location tracking.

8. Notifications, morning alarm and widgets

Daily-goal and prayer reminders are optional local notifications, without a remote push-token service. Preferences and goals stay on the device. When iOS permits background renewal, the plan uses the already saved place, not a new location measurement. Manage lock-screen previews in iPhone settings.

Tapping the optional evening invitation alone does not set an alarm. A morning alarm requires separate confirmation and system alarm permission on supported iOS versions. Turning off invitations does not cancel an already confirmed alarm; cancel it separately in Notifications. Alarm/invitation information is not sent to the social account or progress backup.

Each widget has its own place selection and computes times in its extension without calendar downloads or requiring an app launch. No new App Group or account sharing is used. Tapping a verse opens it in Tarık. Lock-screen content may be visible to others. iOS controls refresh timing; exact boundary updates are not guaranteed.

9. External audio, links and support

When you choose an example recitation, its audio file is requested from AlQuran Cloud / Islamic Network CDN. The provider may see your IP, requested verse/audio address and connection information, which can reveal religious interests. Streaming requires the internet. Library source links also open an external site when selected.

If you send support email, Gmail carries your address, message and attachments to us. We use them to respond and resolve problems. Do not send passwords, sign-in links, raw audio or sensitive location.

When hosted on GitHub Pages, this site is subject to GitHub’s IP logging for security. It adds no analytics/ad cookies, tracking scripts, external fonts, forms or app sign-in. GitHub Pages information.

10. Retention, deletion and data requests

Local learning data remains until deleted on the device. To request account deletion in the app: Profile → Tarık account → Delete my account, type SİL, then choose the permanent-delete button. The sign-in account and linked social profile, friendships, league, shared-goal and cloud-backup records are removed. Safety reports may remain without the account link. This does not delete your Google or Apple account. If you cannot access your account, email support; a safe verification route can be arranged without asking for your password.

Account deletion does not remove local learning history or local measurements. Delete those separately in Settings → Delete learning data on this device. That reset does not remove notification/alarm preferences or the social session; manage these separately. Privacy and help → View my account data retrieves your server-side account export. File and cloud backups are managed separately.

Exact retention periods for Supabase infrastructure logs/backups, Google/Apple/CDN connection logs, GitHub security logs and support email have not been verified in this release preparation. They depend on provider terms and service settings. We do not promise an unverified number of days or instant erasure from all infrastructure backups.

11. Providers, international processing and changes

Supabase, Google, Apple, audio providers, GitHub and email infrastructure may process information outside your country. Applicable legal grounds, processor agreements and transfer arrangements require a separate pre-release assessment; this notice does not assert that assessment is complete. Cloud backup of sensitive learning data requires a separate optional choice.

Provider notices: Supabase, Google, Apple. We update this notice if data flows change. Contact tarikkayet@gmail.com with questions or requests.
