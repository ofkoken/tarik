Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

Reading, lessons and the library work without an account. Account/social access is limited in this version to users declaring they are at least 18.

Does it work offline?

Bundled texts and prayer-time calculations for Türkiye work offline. Sign-in, social features, cloud backup and streamed recitation need the internet. Speech recognition also requires a supported device and language assets.

How do I delete my account?

In the Turkish app open Profil → Tarık hesabım → Hesabımı sil. Type SİL and choose Hesabımı kalıcı sil. Your Google/Apple account itself is not deleted. If sign-in is unavailable, contact support without sending your password.

How do I delete local data?

Ayarlar → Cihazdaki öğrenme verilerini sil → confirm. Local progress, bookmarks and measurements are removed; delete the cloud account/backup separately. Manage notifications and alarms separately in Bildirimler.

What does an uncertain word mean?

Recognition did not confidently match the word. It does not mean your recitation was wrong. Word-text matching is not tajweed or pronunciation assessment.

Why can a widget or reminder be late?

iOS controls refresh and delivery. Check permission, Focus and silent-mode settings. Choose the widget’s location in its own settings. Times are calculated, not an official Diyanet timetable.

What should a bug report include?

Steps, approximate time, app version, device/iOS details and a screenshot with personal information hidden are enough. Do not send passwords, sign-in links, raw audio or sensitive location. TestFlight feedback is another option.
